# Strange Passing / GPT

A separate procedural runway study, published at the `strange-passing-gpt/` suffix. Preserve the existing root preview and every selected walk frame (0–32).

## Direction

Sportswear meets a Belarusian witch's nocturnal fashion show and improbable front row. One large, readable visitor approaches, performs a transformation beside the walker, and leaves completely. Gouache-like muted fills, contrasting coloured pencil hatching, imperfect double outlines and 12 Hz contour changes unify the new artwork. Scene travel and sprite playback remain smooth.

Eight visitor grammars: a seated leshy buyer wearing striped sportswear; an owl buyer on a folding stool; a moon-antlered deer; an umbrella fish raining upward; a door-shell snail releasing fireflies; a drawn gust of leaves and loose scarf threads; a ribbon creature carrying curiosities; a mushroom jellyfish with dangling keys or seeds. Each encounter varies proportions, palette, ornament count, markings, cargo and route from a stable seed. One visitor appears, performs, leaves, and is followed by a quiet gap before the next. The first visitor is a seated buyer. This is combinatorial generation, not a claim of infinitely many authored species or mathematical non-repetition.

## Acceptance

- Self-contained HTML at `site/strange-passing-gpt/index.html`, with no external runtime dependencies.
- All eight families appear in every eight-encounter block; no adjacent family repeats, including block boundaries.
- Visitors anticipate, perform and exit, with foreground and background staging and readable silhouettes on portrait and landscape screens.
- Stable identities across frames; deterministic seek and new-dream reseeding; bounded work during long playback.
- Pause, pace, new dream and next encounter controls; respect reduced-motion preference on initial load.
- Render and inspect desktop and mobile-sized views, every family, the original 32-to-0 seam and a two-minute live run. Record real-phone and gait limitations accurately.
- Dedicated branch and PR; publish only the new suffix in the existing public preview repository.
