# Visual Review

The final page retains the original walking woman as the central subject. The exact accepted atlas is embedded, and an unlit sprite material preserves its colors. One visitor approaches at a time on pure black.

User feedback corrected the earlier creature-only presentation: the deliverable has no finite video composition or HyperFrames runtime. Variation now changes anatomy over broad ranges, rather than tiny mutations of three repeated silhouettes. Inspect the twelve `variant-*.png` captures together for horizontal ribbon wings, rounded/serrated membranes, narrow bird wings, fan tails, long necks and coiled tails.

The spirit turns toward the walker and reveals a growing crown, the moth opens a display, and the bird banks and releases a filament wake. Narrow layouts reduce visitor size and let visitors pass in front of the lower body so their heads remain readable. Girl and creature identities remain stable during each encounter.

Fur is line geometry; membranes and feathers are triangulated surfaces with analytical coloring. The spirit's face and ears remain stylized and somewhat toy-like. This is a procedural sculpture study, not photoreal animal rendering.

The captured 32-to-0 gait seam preserves the known source pose discontinuity. Physical-phone acceptance remains unverified. Recheck full silhouettes and captions after future morphology or camera changes.
