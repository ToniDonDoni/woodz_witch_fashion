# Nocturne / Creature Theatre

## Style Prompt

A cabinet of nocturnal beasts in an empty black theatre. Recognizable animal anatomy, ivory fur, dark porcelain faces, branching coral antlers, metallic membranes and fine articulated feathers. Creatures are sculptures of threads and lamellae, lit like couture objects. Large negative space; one visitor commands the stage. Tiny museum captions recede at the edges.

The original footage-derived woman remains the central walking subject. Visitors approach and react to her. Broad stochastic anatomy and eight contrasting palettes create new combinations indefinitely. HyperFrames supplies inspiration only; the deliverable is an endless browser page.

## Colors

- `#000000`: pure black stage.
- `#ece5d4`: warm ivory fur and primary text.
- `#bcfae2`: mineral mint emission and membrane highlights.
- `#d6a2ff`: lilac rim light and wing membranes.
- `#f7b37b`: amber antlers and bird filaments.

## Typography

Use local generic serif for the expressive exhibit title; generic monospace for specimen identifiers and controls. No runtime font requests.

## Motion

Left-to-right arrivals and departures share a current. A braking creature turns to inspect the viewer. Its recognition triggers growth of glowing antler branches after a brief held pose. The moth unfolds before a powered flight. The bird banks, coils its tail and releases a filament wake. Flapping, limb articulation and trails express propulsion, not idle wobble. All poses derive from absolute time and immutable seeded anatomy.

## What NOT to Do

- No background gradients, stars or forest scenery in this study.
- No abstract glowing blobs in place of anatomy.
- No billboard textures of creatures, remote models or reference-image tracing.
- No idle breathing or rocking to fill a hold.
- No previous-frame feedback, unseeded details or accumulating scene objects.
